ReadMate 隐私政策

Privacy Policy · 更新日期：2026年11月15日

概述 / Overview

ReadMate（以下简称"本应用"）是一款文字识别与朗读辅助工具。我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

ReadMate is a text recognition and reading assistant app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

  • 不要求账户注册：本应用无需注册或登录，我们不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息。
  • 照片与图片：当您使用"AI 增强识别"或"物体识别"功能时，您选择的图片会经压缩后发送至第三方 AI 服务（Poe API）进行处理，仅用于本次识别，处理后我们不会存储或用于其他目的。
  • 本地处理："经典识别"模式使用设备内置的 Apple Vision 技术，在您的设备本地完成，图片不会上传。
  • 识别历史：识别记录仅保存在您的设备本地（SwiftData），不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 订阅与支付：Pro 订阅通过 Apple App Store（StoreKit）完成，支付信息由 Apple 处理，我们不会接触您的银行卡或支付信息。

No account required. The app does not require registration or login, and we do not collect personal identity information such as your name, email, or phone number.

Photos. When you use "AI Enhanced" or object recognition, your selected image is compressed and sent to a third-party AI service (Poe API) solely for that recognition task. It is not stored or used for any other purpose.

On-device processing. Classic recognition uses Apple Vision on your device, and images are not uploaded.

History. Recognition history is stored only locally on your device.

Subscriptions. Pro subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store (StoreKit); we never see your payment details.

第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

  • Poe API（api.poe.com）：用于 AI 增强识别与物体识别的图片处理。请参阅 Poe 的服务条款与隐私政策。
  • Apple（App Store / StoreKit）：用于应用内购买与订阅管理。请参阅 Apple 的隐私政策。

Poe API (api.poe.com) for AI-powered recognition; Apple (App Store / StoreKit) for in-app purchases and subscription management.

数据安全与保留 / Data Security & Retention

识别记录与设置仅保存在您的设备上。发送给 AI 服务的图片仅用于当次识别任务。您可以随时删除应用以清除所有本地数据。

Recognition history and settings are stored only on your device. Images sent to the AI service are used only for the current recognition task. You can delete the app at any time to remove all local data.

儿童隐私 / Children's Privacy

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下的儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

政策变更 / Changes to This Policy

我们可能会不时更新本政策。更新后的政策将在本页面公布。

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
